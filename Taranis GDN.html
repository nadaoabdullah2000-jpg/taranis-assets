Dear Investor,
The Global Data Network returned -15.2% in July, versus -5.9% for the Russell Growth Index and +0.5% for the MSCI World Index.
Growth stocks de-rated sharply. The first leg came from Korea, where the KOSPI fell roughly 30% on margin calls and forced selling, followed by the Situational Awareness fund's 67% loss and the unwind of its $45bn portfolio, which hit our NeoCloud names.
SpaceX added a sector-specific overhang, falling below its $135 IPO price ahead of its first public earnings and lock-up expiry, with 911.5 million additional shares becoming eligible for sale. With the stock now looking rather cheap, we continue to see a fantastic company at an attractive valuation.
This was multiple compression, not deteriorating earnings, and indices now look less expensive with earnings momentum still strong.
Euronext (+13%), Broadcom, Nvidia and SMCI led the winners; our Korean space holdings detracted. We see no weakness in fundamentals, view July as isolated, and expect the last two months to reverse.
